## Short version (under 500 characters)

> Trusted Trade Agents is an open front door for AI purchasing agents at Valio Aimo. Any agent (Gemini, Claude, an ERP) connects over MCP or OpenAPI, searches the 17.5k-product challenge catalog, gets a signed fixed-price quote and orders, while the customer's own policy decides what runs automatically and what needs a named human. Approvals bind to the quote hash, and every step lands in a signed, hash-chained audit log anyone can verify.

## Title
**Trusted Trade Agents: an open, signed front door for AI purchasing agents**

## Tagline
Let any company's AI agent buy from Valio Aimo, inside rules the customer controls, with every decision signed and explained.

## Problem
Foodservice customers want their AI agents to restock milk, plan event menus and swap out-of-stock items, but a wholesaler can't give an agent its ERP login. Today there is no standard way for an outside agent to look up products, prices and stock, get a binding price, and order, while the customer decides what the agent may do alone and what needs a human. Without that, every integration is bespoke and nobody can prove afterwards what was agreed.

## Solution
One gateway with two open surfaces: an **MCP server** (13 tools) and an **OpenAPI 3.1 REST API**, discoverable from `/.well-known/otap.json`. Behind it:
- **Live lookup (plus automatic keyless replay)** over the live Valio Aimo challenge mock price and availability API and the 17,546-product synthetic catalog, with Finnish/Swedish/English hybrid search (full-text plus Gemini embeddings) and €/L comparison. With an empty `.env` (no `AIMO_API_KEY`, no `GEMINI_API_KEY`), the gateway automatically runs off the bundled replay snapshot and 669-product gzipped challenge catalog subset (`tests/fixtures/snapshot/replay/`: 669 product ids across `2026-11-16`..`2026-11-20`, `served_from: replay`), so `uv run aimo serve`, `make accept`, `scenarios.run_all`, `conformance` and `qa_milk_flow.py` work with zero keys or downloaded `data/`.
- **Good-better-best substitution** that explains each rank. Allergens are tri-state; unknown is never safe.
- **Customer-owned guardrails**: a versioned YAML policy per customer (hotel, school kitchen, hospital) with spend limits, weekly budget, category scope, catalog-verified substitution tiers (verified against the catalog, never trusting the agent's label, with customer-listed pack swaps passing under the spend limit on the final policy `7225d06`), customer policy allergen hard-blocks enforced inside `plan_need` as well as at the order gate, pre-planning quantity validation (`quantity <= 0` -> 4xx before planning), cut-off checks, rolling 7-day standing-order drift (counting policy-placed orders on `7225d06`) and `split_order` detection, `price_jump` (>15%) checks, customer-scoped learned "always allow this swap" approvals (`remember` / `/v1/customers/{id}/learned`), and prompt-injection screening across supplier text, the buyer request (`need.raw_text`), and `product_query` (`injection_screen` surfaced on `PlanLine` and `Candidate` as well as in policy evaluation), each returning a plain-language reason.
- **Signed quotes** (Ed25519 JWS, 15-minute price lock) and **human approval bound to the quote hash**. Agents have no approve tool.
- **A signed, hash-chained audit trail** rendered as readable sentences, with chain verification and an offline evidence pack ([docs/evidence/live-run/README.md](evidence/live-run/README.md)).
- A **Gemini buyer agent** as the reference client and an approval UI for the human; when `GEMINI_API_KEY` is absent, a deterministic regex parser and scripted buyer fallback run the same MCP tool flow so `uv run aimo demo` finishes end to end.

Demo: *"We need 1,000 litres of lactose-free milk at Hotel Aurora Helsinki by Monday."* The usual 10 L box missed its cut-off (live mock-API data), so the agent plans Valio Eila 1 L at €1.01/L, €1,010 in total. The hotel's policy says **needs approval** for two reasons on the final policy (`7225d06`): over €500 and 3.3× the usual weekly volume. The 1 L pack is the hotel's listed alternative, so the swap itself is allowed; a listed swap under the limit is placed without a human, while another brand still needs one (pre-`7225d06` runs also flagged the pack swap as a third reason). The procurement manager approves the exact signed quote, the order is placed in our mock order service (no upstream order API) with a Peppol-style "AP accepted" response, and the audit chain verifies.

## How we meet the core requirements
1. **Product, price and availability lookup in structured form.** `search_products`, `get_product`, `get_offers` (MCP) and `/v1/products/search`, `/v1/products/{id}`, `/v1/offers` (REST) return typed JSON: GTIN, sales unit, content per unit, claims, tri-state allergens, delivery reliability, CO2, live price and stock, cut-off time, and an `issues` list (`cutoff_passed`, `partial_stock`, `out_of_stock`, ...) with provenance (live or cached, fetched at).
2. **Inquiry, then an order at fixed prices.** Pre-planning validation rejects non-positive or malformed quantities (`quantity <= 0` -> 4xx before planning; fractional count units `2.5 ST` -> 422 `fractional_quantity`; `> 1e6` -> 422); `plan_need` turns a valid need into a split plan; `create_quote` re-checks price and stock and returns a signed quote with a 15-minute price lock; `place_order` must present the quote id and hash. Unavailable lines are refused with per-line reasons (409), so the agent re-plans instead of silently getting less. If the 15-min quote lock expires, price moves, or stock at approval time falls below the quoted quantity (`reason: partial_stock`) while waiting for human approval, `POST /v1/approvals/{id}/decision` returns `409 requote_needed` with an old-vs-new diff, a fresh signed quote for the human and a pending order (`order.requoted` in audit; see [docs/DEMO.md](DEMO.md)) instead of placing a partial order.
3. **Permissions and human approval.** Every mutating `/v1` route, catalog search, MCP tool and buyer run enforces a known customer and a mandate stating who may act (scopes `catalog:read quote:create order:submit`); the customer's policy says what runs automatically, with catalog-verified substitution tiers, the customer's allergen hard-block enforced directly inside `plan_need` as well as at the order gate, rolling 7-day standing-order drift and `split_order` detection, `price_jump` (>15%) checks, and prompt-injection screening across supplier text, `need.raw_text`, and `product_query` (`injection_screen` surfaced on `PlanLine` and `Candidate` as well as in the order decision). Outcomes are auto-approve, needs approval by a named role, or reject, and the strictest rule wins. Approvals are human-only: distinct 12-char passphrases are generated per directory approver into `data/keys/approver_pins.json` (mode `0600`, overridable via `AIMO_APPROVER_PINS`, with Anna's primary credential mirrored into `data/keys/approver_pin` for local CLI scripts and `AIMO_APPROVER_PIN` kept as single-credential override), and the approver signs in via `POST /v1/auth/approver-session` for a signed 8-hour `X-Approver-Session` bound to that person and credential (brute-force lockout is per `(approver_id, client)`: `5` wrong guesses -> 429 `approver_locked` for 5 min, `30` per 10 min per-approver backstop, so guessing one approver never locks out other approvers or active `X-Approver-Session` tokens; production: the customer's SSO). A decision is accepted only from a named person in the customer's approver directory acting in the right role, for the exact quote hash, before the lock expires and while quoted stock holds (otherwise `409 requote_needed`, including `reason: partial_stock`, issues a fresh signed quote and pending order instead of placing a partial order). Any request carrying agent identity gets 403 `agent_cannot_approve` even if it names a real approver, MCP has no approval tool at all, and every attempt (including refused ones) is audited as `approval.refused`. When a human approves a recurring swap with `remember`, the gateway records a customer-scoped learned approval (`/v1/customers/{id}/learned`). Tier and substitution flags are computed by the gateway, not taken from the agent. An agent also cannot move the clock past a cut-off, fake stock, or start hosted runs anonymously: `X-Aimo-Now` and `X-Aimo-Faults` are ignored on any request carrying agent identity (REST and MCP) unless the operator starts the gateway with `AIMO_DEMO_FAULTS=1` (and never honoured with `AIMO_ENV=production`); starting, answering and listing hosted agent runs (`POST /v1/agent/runs` and sub-routes) require a signed-in human (`X-Approver-Session` or `X-Approver-Pin`, 401 otherwise; in `/ui`, **Run agent** opens the sign-in panel first and the same session covers approval) and ignore body `faults` plus caller-chosen `inproc`/`fake` transports unless `AIMO_DEMO_FAULTS=1`.

## Optional requirements
- **Delivery time and sustainability.** Each product shows the delivery reliability and category CO2 (kg CO2e/kg) from the challenge CSVs; cut-off times and requested dates come from the live API. Organic and origin labels (e.g. Finnish Blue Swan) are parsed from the data.
- **Substitution and exceptions.** Good-better-best ranking with reasons; cut-off passed, partial stock, stock-out, sales block, price shock, upstream 429/503 (retry, then a clear 503 with `Retry-After`). If stock at approval time falls below the quoted quantity, approval returns 409 `requote_needed` (reason `partial_stock`) with a fresh signed quote for the human instead of placing a partial order. Injection text in supplier data, the buyer request (`need.raw_text`), and `product_query` is screened and flagged. Operator-only fault injection (`X-Aimo-Now` and `X-Aimo-Faults` are ignored on any request carrying agent identity over REST and MCP unless the operator starts the gateway with `AIMO_DEMO_FAULTS=1`, and never honoured with `AIMO_ENV=production`) makes every exception demonstrable.
- **Traceability and explainability.** Hash-chained, Ed25519-signed audit events with a readable report per order, `GET /v1/audit/verify`, and an evidence pack (events, signed quote, decision, JWKS, verification steps) for offline checking.
- **Scenarios covered:** last-minute event, supplier stock-out with a compatible substitute (Gemini recipe check), allergen-safe substitution for a school (milk and nuts) and a hospital (gluten), prompt injection in supplier text, standing-order drift (3× usual volume). Six of these run live end to end with the Gemini agent and all six pass (see Robustness). Menu-to-order (`POST /v1/recipes/plan`, MCP `list_recipes` / `plan_recipe`, live acceptance scenarios `s2`, `s3`, `s5`) and proactive selling (`POST /v1/proposals`, MCP `propose_offer`, live scenario `s6`) pass in `scenarios.run_all` (8/8 PASS) and are evaluated as `menu_recipe` and `upsell_pitch` (40 Gemini-written scenarios each, 0 policy and 0 allergen violations; [windtunnel-results.md](windtunnel-results.md)).

## Tech
Python 3.11, FastAPI, Pydantic v2, SQLite (FTS5 trigram), official `mcp` SDK (streamable HTTP and stdio), `google-genai`, `cryptography` (Ed25519), PyYAML; no-build HTML/JS approval UI. Gemini: `gemini-3.8-flash` (buyer agent, need parsing, scenario generation), `gemini-4-hydrogen` with high thinking (substitution decisions), `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` (allergen inference over 4,076 products, recipe check, injection screen), `gemini-embedding-2` (17,546-product hybrid search). About 31% of the agent's input tokens are served from Gemini's implicit cache on the committed after-trim ledger (38% on the Sun 02:54 final-code montage). If a Gemini model's daily quota runs out (this happened live on Sun 15 Nov 05:00 with `gemini-3.8-flash`), `aimo.gemini.generate` falls back at once to the next model in `GEMINI_FALLBACK_MODELS` (default `gemini-3.5-flash`, then `gemini-4-hydrogen`), skips the exhausted model for 30 minutes, and logs the model that answered (`meta.fallback_from`); the hero run then placed its order in 29 s on `gemini-3.5-flash` (the committed montage numbers were measured on `gemini-3.8-flash`). One command: `uv run aimo serve`.

## Openness and production path
Open standards throughout: MCP, OpenAPI 3.1, JWS/EdDSA (RFC 7515, 8037) with a JWKS, RFC 7638 key ids, canonical JSON close to RFC 8785, GS1 GTINs, Peppol BIS order-response codes (AP/CA/RE), EU14 allergens (EU FIC 1169/2011). Production path: a SAP/webshop adapter behind the existing `BackendClient` interface; OAuth 2.1 per the MCP authorization spec with RFC 9396 authorization details; mandates as W3C Verifiable Credentials; signing keys in a KMS; Peppol BIS Ordering over the Peppol network; audit events in an append-only WORM store with anchored chain heads; policies portable to OPA or Cedar. Every assumption is listed with its impact and production replacement in [ASSUMPTIONS.md](ASSUMPTIONS.md) (no upstream order API, invented customers, inferred categories, mock-API quirks we measured); every design choice has an ADR with rejected alternatives in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md); every number has a reproducing command in [NUMBERS.md](NUMBERS.md); the phased plan (3-customer pilot → SAP adapter → OAuth/VC mandates → KMS → Peppol network) with owners and risks is in [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) §5.
