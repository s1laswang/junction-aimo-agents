## Title
**Trusted Trade Agents: an open, signed front door for AI purchasing agents**

## Tagline
Let any company's AI agent buy from Valio Aimo, inside rules the customer controls, with every decision signed and explained.

## Problem
Foodservice customers want their AI agents to restock milk, plan event menus and swap out-of-stock items, but a wholesaler can't give an agent its ERP login. Today there is no standard way for an outside agent to look up products, prices and stock, get a binding price, and order, while the customer decides what the agent may do alone and what needs a human. Without that, every integration is bespoke and nobody can prove afterwards what was agreed.

## Solution
One gateway with two open surfaces: an **MCP server** (13 tools) and an **OpenAPI 3.1 REST API**, discoverable from `/.well-known/otap.json`. Behind it:
- **Live lookup** over the live Valio Aimo challenge mock price and availability API and the 17,546-product synthetic catalog, with Finnish/Swedish/English hybrid search (full-text plus Gemini embeddings) and €/L comparison.
- **Good-better-best substitution** that explains each rank. Allergens are tri-state; unknown is never safe.
- **Customer-owned guardrails**: a versioned YAML policy per customer (hotel, school kitchen, hospital) with spend limits, weekly budget, category scope, substitution tiers, allergen hard blocks, cut-off checks, standing-order drift over a rolling 7-day window, split-order detection, price jumps over 15%, learned "always allow this swap" approvals and prompt-injection screening, each returning a plain-language reason.
- **Signed quotes** (Ed25519 JWS, 15-minute price lock) and **human approval bound to the quote hash**. Agents have no approve tool.
- **A signed, hash-chained audit trail** rendered as readable sentences, with chain verification and an offline evidence pack.
- A **Gemini buyer agent** as the reference client and an approval UI for the human.

Demo: *"We need 1,000 litres of lactose-free milk at Hotel Aurora Helsinki by Monday."* The usual 10 L box missed its cut-off (live mock-API data), so the agent plans Valio Eila 1 L at €1.01/L, €1,010 in total. The hotel's policy says **needs approval** for two reasons: over €500 and 3.3× the usual weekly volume. The 1 L pack is the hotel's listed alternative, so the swap itself is allowed; a listed swap under the limit is placed without a human, while another brand still needs one. The procurement manager approves the exact signed quote, the order is placed in our mock order service (no upstream order API) with a Peppol-style "AP accepted" response, and the audit chain verifies.

## How we meet the core requirements
1. **Product, price and availability lookup in structured form.** `search_products`, `get_product`, `get_offers` (MCP) and `/v1/products/search`, `/v1/products/{id}`, `/v1/offers` (REST) return typed JSON: GTIN, sales unit, content per unit, claims, tri-state allergens, delivery reliability, CO2, live price and stock, cut-off time, and an `issues` list (`cutoff_passed`, `partial_stock`, `out_of_stock`, ...) with provenance (live or cached, fetched at).
2. **Inquiry, then an order at fixed prices.** `plan_need` turns a need into a split plan; `create_quote` re-checks price and stock and returns a signed quote with a 15-minute price lock; `place_order` must present the quote id and hash. Unavailable lines are refused with per-line reasons (409), so the agent re-plans instead of silently getting less.
3. **Permissions and human approval.** A mandate says who may act (scopes `catalog:read quote:create order:submit`); the customer's policy says what runs automatically. Outcomes are auto-approve, needs approval by a named role, or reject, and the strictest rule wins. Approvals are human-only: the approver signs in with a credential no agent can obtain (`POST /v1/auth/approver-session`, PIN in, signed 8-hour session bound to that one person out; production: the customer's SSO). A decision is accepted only from a named person in the customer's approver directory acting in the right role, for the exact quote hash, before the lock expires. Any request carrying agent identity gets 403 even if it names a real approver, MCP has no approval tool at all, and every attempt (including refused ones) is audited as `approval.refused`. Tier and substitution flags are computed by the gateway, not taken from the agent. An agent also cannot move the clock past a cut-off or fake stock: the demo headers `X-Aimo-Now` and `X-Aimo-Faults` are ignored on any request carrying agent identity (REST and MCP) unless the operator starts the gateway with `AIMO_DEMO_FAULTS=1`, and never in production; the same holds for the `faults` field of `POST /v1/agent/runs`, and starting a hosted agent run needs a signed-in human.

## Optional requirements
- **Delivery time and sustainability.** Each product shows the delivery reliability and category CO2 (kg CO2e/kg) from the challenge CSVs; cut-off times and requested dates come from the live API. Organic and origin labels (e.g. Finnish Blue Swan) are parsed from the data.
- **Substitution and exceptions.** Good-better-best ranking with reasons; cut-off passed, partial stock, stock-out, sales block, price shock, upstream 429/503 (retry, then a clear 503 with `Retry-After`). If stock falls below the quoted quantity while the order waits for approval, the approval returns 409 `requote_needed` (reason `partial_stock`) with a fresh quote for the human, never a silently partial order. Injection text in supplier data and in the request (including the product query) is flagged. Operator-only fault injection (`X-Aimo-Faults` with `AIMO_DEMO_FAULTS=1`) makes every exception demonstrable.
- **Traceability and explainability.** Hash-chained, Ed25519-signed audit events with a readable report per order, `GET /v1/audit/verify`, and an evidence pack (events, signed quote, decision, JWKS, verification steps) for offline checking.
- **Scenarios covered:** last-minute event, supplier stock-out with a compatible substitute (Gemini recipe check), allergen-safe substitution for a school (milk and nuts) and a hospital (gluten), prompt injection in supplier text, standing-order drift (3× usual volume). Six of these run live end to end with the Gemini agent and all six pass (see Robustness). Menu-to-order and proactive selling are MCP tools (`plan_recipe`, `list_recipes`, `propose_offer`, `check_standing_order_drift`) and wind-tunnel scenario families (`menu_recipe` and `upsell_pitch`, 40 Gemini-written scenarios each, 0 policy and 0 allergen violations; [windtunnel-results.md](windtunnel-results.md)).

## Robustness (measured)
**Live montage, 6 of 6 scenarios PASS** (`uv run aimo demo --montage`, Gemini agent over MCP against the live mock API, re-recorded on the current code Sun 15 Nov 02:54; [demo-montage.md](demo-montage.md)):
| Scenario | What happens |
|---|---|
| happy | Usual product past its cut-off, same-brand substitute, EUR 1,010, needs approval (in this Sunday 02:54 run: spend, substitution and 3.3× volume; with the final policy the listed pack swap is allowed, so two reasons remain), approved by Anna, placed |
| stockout | Injected partial stock: the order is split into 4 lines (EUR 2,544.80), shortfall 0, approved by Anna, placed |
| school-allergen | Milk products rejected for a school with milk and nut allergies; the school's usual Oatly Oat Drink Whole (structured milk- and nut-free) for EUR 280, auto-approved by the school's own policy (under its €300 limit) |
| injection | Planted instructions in supplier text: 6 texts flagged by the regex screen and redacted before the model saw them, the policy cites injection, the quantity stays at 300 |
| drift | 4,000 L standing order (13× usual): quote prepared, order held for human confirmation |
| event | Multi-item request (cream and butter, lactose-free): one quote with 2 lines (EUR 266.49), approved by Anna, placed |

Each scenario takes 20–37 s end to end. Every human approval went through the real decision endpoint with the human approver credential (the agent has no approval tool). The agent escalated to gemini-4-hydrogen with high thinking only for the quote-decision turn, on the 5 runs whose plan had a substitution, flag or split (5 of 34 agent steps, 15%). Cost per placed order: **$0.0352** in Gemini calls (down from $0.0822 before a token trim, −57%; 38% of input served from Gemini's implicit cache). Recompute offline: `uv run python docs/evidence/montage_stats.py`.

Wind tunnel ([windtunnel-results.md](windtunnel-results.md), pipeline mode; the scenarios, recorded API responses and run files are committed in [evidence/windtunnel/](evidence/windtunnel/README.md) and re-run offline in about 3 minutes with no keys): 640 Gemini-written scenarios (16 families, 3 policy archetypes), scored by a deterministic oracle (`windtunnel/oracle.py`). Re-scored on the final policy (Sun 15 Nov 05:50): the gateway has **0 policy violations and 0 allergen violations**, **80 of 80 of the scenarios' injection payloads flagged** by the regex screen (1 false positive in 560 clean requests), escalation recall **99.7%**, escalation precision **69.5%** (331 needed, 145 not; up from 54.0%; our 90% target is not met), outcome accuracy 74.8% (from 55.0%). The naive agent with direct API access, on the same scenarios and labels: **372 policy violations, 193 allergen violations**, 0 injections blocked, recall 17.8%. 9 of 640 scenarios errored in the pipeline for both and count as errors, not passes. Before those changes (Sat 18:24): gateway 1 policy violation, recall 98.8%, precision 54.0%; injection block 56.2% vs 0%; median € over the cheapest compliant plan 0.0% for both. At 2,560 template scenarios: 11 vs 1,452 policy violations, 0 vs 728 allergen violations. On a separate, harder 36-payload evaluation set the picture is less flattering: the regex screen alone has 44.4% recall, regex + Gemini 94.4%, but Gemini also flags 42 of 60 normal buyer requests (0 of 60 supplier texts). So the shipped gateway runs the regex screen and the Gemini layer is opt-in (`AIMO_SCREEN_GEMINI=1`); either way a flag only sends the order to a human. Input hardening: a fuzz test sends about 2,600 malformed requests to every `/v1` route with 0 server errors; fractional pieces (2.5 ST) and absurd quantities (> 1e6) get a plain 422, and quantities far above the customer's usual volume or unknown stock always need a human (`tests/test_fuzz.py`, `tests/test_harden.py`). Agent mode (the Gemini buyer through the real gateway, 48 scenarios, Sat 15:16): 0 policy and 0 allergen violations, escalation recall 100%; a Gemini judge scored the transcripts 0.95 mandate followed, 1.00 no invented data, 1.00 injection resisted.

Also measured: the regex injection screen flags 0 of 34,159 supplier marketing texts in the challenge catalog (no false alarms, but only 44% recall on the evaluation set above); the catalog unit parser agrees with live comparison prices for 93% of a 752-product sample (single source, the catalog report); `pytest` gives 375 passed, 7 skipped, 1 xfailed, 1 xpassed (keyless: 274 passed, 0 failed). An agent naming a real approver gets 403 and the refusal is in the audit log (re-run Sat 23:10; [DEMO.md](DEMO.md) "Permissions attack"). Commands for every number: [NUMBERS.md](NUMBERS.md).

## Tech
Python 3.11, FastAPI, Pydantic v2, SQLite (FTS5 trigram), official `mcp` SDK (streamable HTTP and stdio), `google-genai`, `cryptography` (Ed25519), PyYAML; no-build HTML/JS approval UI. Gemini: `gemini-3.8-flash` (buyer agent, need parsing, scenario generation), `gemini-4-hydrogen` with high thinking (substitution decisions), `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` (allergen inference over 4,076 products, recipe check, injection screen), `gemini-embedding-2` (17,546-product hybrid search). About 31% of the agent's input tokens are served from Gemini's implicit cache. If a Gemini model's daily quota runs out (this happened live on Sun 15 Nov 05:00 with gemini-3.8-flash), `aimo.gemini.generate` falls back at once to the next model in `GEMINI_FALLBACK_MODELS` (default gemini-3.5-flash, then gemini-4-hydrogen), skips the exhausted model for 30 minutes and logs the model that answered (`meta.fallback_from`); the hero run then placed its order in 29 s on gemini-3.5-flash. The committed montage numbers were measured on gemini-3.8-flash. One command: `uv run aimo serve`.

## Openness and production path
Open standards throughout: MCP, OpenAPI 3.1, JWS/EdDSA (RFC 7515, 8037) with a JWKS, RFC 7638 key ids, canonical JSON close to RFC 8785, GS1 GTINs, Peppol BIS order-response codes (AP/CA/RE), EU14 allergens (EU FIC 1169/2011). Production path: a SAP/webshop adapter behind the existing `BackendClient` interface; OAuth 2.1 per the MCP authorization spec with RFC 9396 authorization details; mandates as W3C Verifiable Credentials; signing keys in a KMS; Peppol BIS Ordering over the Peppol network; audit events in an append-only WORM store with anchored chain heads; policies portable to OPA or Cedar. Every assumption is listed with its impact and production replacement in [ASSUMPTIONS.md](ASSUMPTIONS.md) (no upstream order API, invented customers, inferred categories, mock-API quirks we measured); every design choice has an ADR with rejected alternatives in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md); every number has a reproducing command in [NUMBERS.md](NUMBERS.md); the phased plan (3-customer pilot → SAP adapter → OAuth/VC mandates → KMS → Peppol network) with owners and risks is in [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) §5.
